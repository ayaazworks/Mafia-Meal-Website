Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how AW Innovate ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the mobile game Mafia Meal (the "App"). By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information We Do Not Collect Directly

Mafia Meal does not require you to create an account, and we do not directly ask you to provide your name, email address, or other personal contact details to play the game.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically via Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information used to identify your device, as described below.

1.3 Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements, including interstitial ads, within the App. AdMob may collect and process data such as:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • IP address and general/approximate location
  • Device information (device model, operating system, language, screen size)
  • App usage and interaction data (e.g., ad impressions, clicks)

This data is used to serve and measure ads, including personalized advertising where permitted, and to prevent fraud. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's own privacy policy. You can learn more and manage your ad personalization settings at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from partner sites/apps
  • Google Ads Settings (manage ad personalization)

1.4 In-App Purchase Data (Google Play Billing)

If the App offers in-app purchases, these purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, view, or store your payment card details, billing address, or other payment credentials — these are handled directly by Google Play in accordance with Google's own privacy and payment terms. We may receive limited, non-financial confirmation that a purchase or transaction occurred (such as a purchase token or product ID) in order to unlock the corresponding in-app content.

1.5 Device and Diagnostic Data

Like most mobile apps, basic diagnostic information (such as crash logs, App version, and general device type) may be collected to help us fix bugs and improve stability. This information is not used to personally identify you.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App
  • To display and measure advertising through Google AdMob
  • To process in-app purchases through Google Play Billing
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues and crashes
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Children's Privacy

Mafia Meal is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner inconsistent with applicable law, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) where applicable. If our App is configured for or accessed by children, advertising is served in accordance with Google AdMob's policies for child-directed treatment, which may limit or disable personalized advertising and certain data collection. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information in a manner not permitted by law, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

5. Data Retention

We do not maintain our own user account database for the App. Any data processed by third-party services (AdMob, Google Play Billing) is retained according to those providers' own retention practices, as described in their respective privacy policies.

6. Your Choices

  • You can reset or limit your device's Advertising ID in your device's system settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time to stop all data collection associated with it.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed in connection with the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Users

If you use the App from outside India, your information (as processed by our third-party providers such as Google) may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, in accordance with those providers' privacy policies.
