About the Game

Mafia Meal is a quick-reflex party game for solo play or with friends and family. Your plate is full of peas and your hand is quick — but a watchful gangster is sitting across the table with a newspaper. Eat as many peas as you can while he's reading. The moment he lowers the paper and looks up, freeze instantly — if he catches your hand still moving, he shoots, and you're out!
